# Game Design Summary

## Premise

The player protects a young life-tree from forest creatures corrupted by an expanding mist. Defensive plants and woodland helpers are placed around fixed enemy routes.

## Core loop

1. Inspect the route and available build slots.
2. Spend sunlight to plant a tower.
3. Start a wave and defeat enemies automatically.
4. Upgrade, specialize, or sell towers.
5. Use active skills to recover from dangerous moments.
6. Protect the sprout through the final boss wave.
7. Earn stars and unlock the next stage.

## Initial tower roster

- Pea Tower: inexpensive single-target physical damage
- Mushroom Lamp: area magic damage and poison
- Ice Flower: slow, freeze, and area control
- Thorn Vine: ground-only line attacks and bleeding
- Bee Hive: tracking summoned units and poison

## Initial enemy roster

- Beetle: baseline unit
- Jump Mushroom: fast, low-health unit
- Stone Beast: slow armored unit
- Corrupted Slime: splits on death
- Moth: flying unit
- Healing Spirit: supports nearby enemies
- Forest Golem: multi-phase boss

## Differentiators

- Destructible map objects that reveal coins or build slots
- Plant combinations and aura interactions
- Multiple upgrade branches
- Day/night and terrain modifiers in later chapters
